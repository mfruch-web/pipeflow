How to play

Get water from the tank to every house, with no leaks.

  • Tap a tile to turn it a quarter turn.
  • Pipes connected to the tank fill with water right away.
  • Hold a tile (or right-click it) to lock it so you don't bump it by accident.
  • You win when every pipe is connected and none point off the edge or into a wall.
  • Stuck? Hint puts one tile in place (+5 taps), or Give up shows the solution.

Scoring

Each puzzle has a par: the fewest taps it takes to solve it. You're scored on time and on how many taps over par you take, like golf. Each hint adds 5 taps.

Your share squares show how close you came to par: 🟩🟩🟩🟩 is perfect, and each 🟨 means more taps over par (up to 10%, 25%, 50%, then 100% over).

Every day

Everyone gets the same three puzzles each day: Easy, Medium and Hard.
